# Limitations

- Wayfinder does not book travel.
- Wayfinder does not guarantee live prices.
- Wayfinder does not guarantee business hours.
- Budget estimates are approximate.
- Place data comes from open data sources and may be incomplete.
- Users should verify reservations, hours, transportation, and costs manually.
- The demo should not collect payment details.
- The demo should not collect sensitive personal information unnecessarily.
